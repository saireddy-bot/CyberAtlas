\# CyberAtlas



\## Cybersecurity Risk \& Security Posture Management Platform



CyberAtlas is a cybersecurity software platform designed to help organizations identify security risks, assess their potential business impact, prioritize critical findings, and support remediation.



The platform follows a continuous security workflow:



Asset Discovery → Security Assessment → Risk Analysis → Business Impact → Risk Prioritization → Remediation → Reassessment



\## Key Features



\- Asset inventory and security assessment

\- Vulnerability and security finding analysis

\- Risk scoring

\- Business impact assessment

\- Risk prioritization

\- Remediation guidance

\- Security incident management

\- Alert management

\- Asset availability monitoring

\- Exposure analysis

\- Security posture tracking

\- Security reassessment

\## Project Objective



CyberAtlas aims to provide organizations with a centralized view of their cybersecurity posture and help security teams understand:



\- Which assets are at risk?

\- Which risks are most critical?

\- What could happen if a risk is exploited?

\- Which risks should be addressed first?

\- How can the identified risk be remediated?

\- Has the security posture improved after remediation?
